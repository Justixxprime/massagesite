Three Stretches to
Prepare for Our Time

Journal Article Written by Emily

If your body feels tight from the work week, you're carrying tension that will get in the way of fully surrendering to pleasure. Hours of sitting and stress pull your shoulders forward and lock up your hips—the very areas that need to be open and receptive for our time together.

For every bit of tension you hold in your hips and lower back, your ability to fully relax into sensation diminishes. Most people arrive carrying a week's worth of stress without realizing it, which is why taking twenty minutes to prepare makes such a difference in how deeply you can feel.

These three stretches take under five minutes each and can be done the morning of our date, no equipment needed. They open the areas that matter most for intimate connection.

Hip Flexor Release

Kneel on one knee with the other foot forward, creating a gentle stretch through the front of your hip. Tilt your pelvis slightly forward and hold for 30 seconds per side. This directly counters the sitting position that tightens the hips all week, and it's the single most important stretch for being fully present in your body during our time.

Shoulder and Chest Opener

Stand in a doorway and place your forearms on the frame at shoulder height, then gently lean forward until you feel a stretch across your chest and the front of your shoulders. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds. This opens the posture that tends to close off when we're stressed, allowing you to breathe deeper and receive touch more fully.

Seated Forward Fold

Sit with legs extended and hinge at the hips to reach toward your toes, keeping your back straight rather than rounded. Hold for 30 seconds. This releases the lower back and hamstrings, creating length through the entire back body so you can lie back comfortably without fidgeting.

The timing

Do these the morning of our date, or at least a few hours before. Stretching immediately beforehand can leave muscles too relaxed and slightly uncoordinated. The goal is to arrive feeling open, aware, and fully inhabiting your body—not rushed or still carrying Friday's stress.

None of these replace the touch you'll receive from me, but arriving with your body already warmed up and receptive allows you to sink into pleasure faster and stay there longer.
